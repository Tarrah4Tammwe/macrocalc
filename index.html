Common Questions

How accurate is this calculator?

We use the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, the gold standard for estimating daily caloric needs. Your personal accuracy depends on your metabolism, genetics, and training history. We recommend tracking for 2–3 weeks and adjusting based on real outcomes — that's where true personalisation happens.

Why don't my macros match someone else's?

Because you're different. A 60kg runner and a 100kg lifter need completely different amounts. Two people with the same weight but different activity levels also differ significantly. Generic advice fails because it ignores these differences. Personal calculation doesn't.

How often should I recalculate?

Every 4–8 weeks, or whenever your weight changes by 5% or more. Your body adapts as you train, so your caloric needs shift. A monthly check-in keeps you on track for long-term goals.

Do I need to count macros strictly?

No. Think of these as targets, not rules. Most people find macro awareness helpful without obsessive tracking. Even hitting them within 10g is effective. The goal is awareness and consistency, not perfection.
