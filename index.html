Common Questions

How accurate is this calculator?

We use the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, the gold standard in nutrition science for estimating daily caloric needs. Your personal accuracy depends on metabolism, genetics, and training history. We recommend tracking for 2–3 weeks and adjusting based on real outcomes — that's where personalisation truly happens.

What if I have dietary restrictions?

This calculator gives you your macro targets. How you hit them is entirely up to you. Vegan, vegetarian, keto, carnivore, Mediterranean — pick any foods that fit your targets. Macros work the same regardless of where they come from.

How often should I recalculate?

Every 4–8 weeks, or whenever your weight changes by 5% or more. Your body adapts as you train and your body composition shifts, so your caloric needs change. A monthly check-in keeps you on track for long-term goals.

Why don't my macros match someone else's targets?

Because you're different. A 60kg runner and a 100kg lifter need completely different calorie and macro amounts. Two people with the same weight but different activity levels also differ significantly. Generic advice fails because it ignores these differences. Personal calculation doesn't.

Is protein really that important?

For muscle maintenance and building, yes. During weight loss, high protein preserves muscle mass while you're in a deficit. During muscle building, it maximises muscle protein synthesis. During recovery, it repairs training damage. Protein is the one macro you can't really compromise on for any active goal.
