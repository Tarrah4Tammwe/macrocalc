Common Questions

How accurate are these calculations?

Our calculator uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, the gold standard for estimating daily caloric needs. Individual results may vary based on metabolism, genetics, and training history. We recommend tracking results for 2-3 weeks and adjusting as needed.

Can I change my macros later?

Absolutely. Your body adapts over time. We recommend recalculating every 4-8 weeks or whenever your weight changes by more than 5 lbs.

Is this suitable for beginners?

Yes! Our calculator works for all experience levels, from complete beginners to elite athletes. We adjust macro ratios based on your activity level and specific niche goal.

Do I need to count macros strictly?

No. Think of these as targets, not rules. Most people find macro awareness helpful without obsessive tracking. Even hitting them within 10g is effective.

Why does my niche matter for macros?

Different training styles have very different nutritional demands. A keto dieter needs 70% fat and minimal carbs, while an endurance athlete needs 55%+ carbs for sustained energy. Generic calculators miss this entirely.
